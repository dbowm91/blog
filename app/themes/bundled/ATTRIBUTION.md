# Bundled Theme Attribution

## Source

These themes use the Halloy-compatible TOML format, originally designed for
the [Halloy IRC client](https://github.com/squidowl/halloy).

## Credits

Each theme's original author is credited within the corresponding `.toml`
file's `[metadata]` section where present. The themes are community-contributed
and redistributed under their respective licenses.
